Formula Calculator — Shortcuts

Start: Pick the product once — the calculator then proceeds without asking again
F1/F2/F3: Calculate respective formulas
❌: Remove individual calculations
F5: Refresh page
F8: Print calculations
Enter: Navigate between fields
Click Remaining Qty: Copy to Q field
Lot Assignment: When Remaining = 0 the batch auto-assigns to the first free lot (or the lot with the largest balance) — the lot buttons are a manual override
* Std Qty: The Std Qty (A) reference shown in the input-card title
Active Lot: Drop-down beside the Formula Calculator title — starts at Lot-I; lots are never auto-assigned. A/C values that differ between lots are highlighted in light red
