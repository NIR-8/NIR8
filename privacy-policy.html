VAULT Autofill – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

VAULT Autofill is a browser extension that helps you fill usernames and passwords stored in your personal OneKey VAULT.

What data we access

  • The extension reads login forms on websites you visit so it can offer to fill credentials.
  • It connects to the same Firebase project used by your OneKey VAULT web app to retrieve your encrypted vault data.

How your data is handled

  • Zero-knowledge encryption – Your master password never leaves your device. All entries are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The extension only decrypts credentials in memory when you explicitly request a fill.
  • Decrypted passwords exist only temporarily in the extension’s memory and are never written to disk in plain text.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share your passwords, usernames, or browsing history with any third party.

Permissions explained

  • storage – Remember your last username for convenience.
  • activeTab / tabs / scripting – Detect login forms and fill them when you choose to.
  • alarms – Auto-lock the vault after inactivity.
  • Host permissions for Firebase – Required to sync your encrypted vault data.

Contact

This extension is provided as a companion to the OneKey VAULT web application. For questions about privacy, please contact the maintainer of your VAULT instance.
