VAULT Autofill – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

VAULT Autofill is a browser extension that helps you fill usernames and passwords stored in your personal OneKey VAULT.

What data we access

  • The extension reads the username and password fields on the page where you are signing in, only to fill them or to save that login to your personal vault.
  • It connects to the same Firebase project used by your OneKey VAULT web app to retrieve and sync your encrypted vault data.

How your data is handled

  • Encryption on your device – Login data is encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from your master password, before it is sent to your vault in Google Firestore. Your master password and encryption key never leave your device.
  • Local storage – The extension stores your settings, vault username, a list of one-way hashes of site names (to offer “Unlock” on known sites while the vault is locked), and sites you excluded.
  • Temporary in-memory data – The unlocked encryption key and any login waiting to be saved are kept in memory only. They are cleared when the browser closes or after 5 minutes of inactivity.
  • Decrypted credentials exist only temporarily in the extension’s memory and are never written to disk in plain text.
  • No analytics or advertising are used. We do not collect, sell, or share your passwords, usernames, or browsing history.

Permissions explained

  • storage – Remember your settings, vault username, site hashes, and excluded sites locally.
  • activeTab / tabs / scripting – Detect login forms and fill them when you choose to.
  • alarms – Auto-lock the vault after inactivity.
  • Host permissions for Firebase – Required to sync your encrypted vault data.

Contact

This extension is provided as a companion to the OneKey VAULT web application. For questions about privacy, please contact the maintainer of your VAULT instance.
